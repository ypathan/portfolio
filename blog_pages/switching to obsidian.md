As I have talked about before, i have switched from notion to obsidian due to notion having some issues. Previously, i talked about the technical side, on how i have tried different workflows, different deployments. This is just some thoughts on the whole process and journey and how i feel about it.

advantages obsidian gives me that notion did not provide

1. local storage: my notes are in my control
	- i have them on my laptop
	- synced to my macbook 
	- versioned controlled using git to github 
	- uploaded to my vps so that i can share them publicly, specifically my worklogs and work related documents that i share with colleagues

2. Access to AI
	- notion AI was extra payment like 20 dollars or something
	- now i can just use opencode, it provides free models that i can use for simple tasks and i can connect my deepseek api when i need something heavy

3. I had to create my own note sharing workflow apart from notion
	- this taught me about caching (validation, what type of files to cache)
	- using docker mount points
	- nginx performance tuning (workers, threads)
	- although i was asking AI to do, but at least now i have an idea (a rough one) on how these tools work and if i need them i future (just the idea in my head) with some help from AI should be good in pointing me in the right direction


4. i now have something i use on a daily basis hosted on my own server after so long
	- from 2021 when i got introduced to linux, self hosting etc.., 90% for of the time during these 5 years, ive had my own server
	- to play around with stuff
	- but for the first time now i have had a real world use case for it and a dependency on a day to day basis
