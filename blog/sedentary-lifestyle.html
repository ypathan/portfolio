Sedentary Lifestyle and Getting Weak

I think I am getting weaker. There was a time when I preferred being out and about, and I would dislike having to sit around a computer for an entire day. After a full day of work at a computer, I would want to go for a run, do some workouts and get some fresh air.

But now the sedentary lifestyle is getting to me. I would rather stay at my desk, and I no longer enjoy the physical activities I used to. There was a time when I would happily cycle everywhere, but now that is not the case.

As I get older and spend more of my life sedentary, sitting in front of a computer with less and less sunlight and more and more blue light, I don't do much running now, I have stopped playing sports, and I gave up the gym for a while too.

So now I feel like I need to get back into that routine and bring back some level of daily physical activity into my life, whether that be running, the gym, or something else. My fitness has slowly declined, and I would rather catch it early and get back into shape before it becomes a bigger problem.

Moreover, one may say I lack discipline. I can start something in the dopamine rush, but then when push comes to shove I leave everything. Everyone faces stress in one form or another, but if you have a framework in place to deal with that, or not just "deal", but release it, something you love doing every day that can release stress, like sports, it can greatly benefit someone in their life.

Because you cannot control what happens to you, but how you react and handle things is in your control, and you need to have that framework in place before it can get to your head.
